Round 4
Same pixels, one property apart

Glass that bends what is behind it

Every surface in the app was frosted glass, and frosted glass is the wrong material. A blur averages what sits behind a panel; it never moves it. Real glass has thickness, so light entering near the rim bends — content behind an edge is displaced and magnified. That bend is the whole read, and nothing in the app had it.

The only variable between each pair below is the refracting edge. Same blur, same tint, same rim, same shadow.

Before — blur only
After — blur + refracting edge
The header. Three of its four edges are off-screen, so it gets a strip instead of a ring — the row sliding underneath pinches at the boundary. That pinch is what makes it a pane rather than a painted band.
The dock. Two fixes, not one. The wrapper used to paint a paper scrim to fade the list out underneath — which left the glass nothing but flat paper to work on, and a blurred flat gradient is the same flat gradient. The scrim is gone, so the list is the material. The gutter around the pills also grew 6px → 8px, because the refracting band cannot be wider than the padding without covering them.
The toast. This was the screen you pointed at, and it was not glass at all — a solid --popover card with a 1px border. It is the clearest case in the product: a toast always appears over something.
