The spinning wheel

The count is not the subject

At rest the hub answers a real question before you commit: how many places are actually on the wheel today, after exclusions and filters. The moment the camera goes in, that number is spent — you are reading restaurant names now — and at the zoom's 2.3× it became the largest, highest-contrast object on the screen, sitting in the middle of the four names it was competing with.

So it recedes with the camera: the disc shrinks to an axle the wedges converge on, and the count leaves with it. Scale and opacity only, both on the zoom's own timing — the hub travels rather than blinking out and back.
