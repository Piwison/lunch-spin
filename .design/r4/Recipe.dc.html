Anatomy

Four layers, one rule

A cross-section through the rim of any glass surface in the app. Only layer 2 is new — and it is the one that was missing.

1
Blur and tint, on the element
Unchanged: backdrop-filter: blur() saturate() plus a translucent white fill. This is the part that already worked, and the part that was doing all the work alone.
2
The refracting band, on a ring
An SVG feDisplacementMap referenced from backdrop-filter, driven by a map generated per surface from the signed distance field of its own rounded rectangle. It sits on a masked ring so the interior stays a plain blur — and so a browser that ignores filter references loses the bend and nothing else.
3
The specular rim
A 1.25px directional gradient drawn as a ring, bright where light arrives and dark on the shadowed quarter. Not a border.
4
Three shadows
A 1px contact shadow, a short lift, and a long ambient one — a pane resting above the page rather than a rectangle with a drop shadow.
The rule
The pull never exceeds the band.
Measured: drag the backdrop further than the band is wide and the band tears into two visible steps with a seam between them. A lens has no seam. So there is one number per surface — sheets and cards 14px, bars 8px — and the pull equals it.
